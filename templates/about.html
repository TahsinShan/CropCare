About Plant Guardian

Plant Guardian is an innovative system that combines AI and IoT to help users monitor the health of their plants. Upload a photo of your plant and get instant health analysis. Optionally, connect a soil sensor for more precise insights.

Our goal is to empower gardeners and farmers with easy-to-use technology, helping plants thrive and reduce crop loss. 🌱
